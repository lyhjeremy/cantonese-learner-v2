Cantonese Learner V2
today's news, the way it is spoken

A free web app for learning spoken Cantonese

Today's Hong Kong news, rewritten the way an anchor would say it.

Written Chinese and spoken Cantonese differ in vocabulary and grammar: 認為 on the page is 覺得 out loud. Every morning a GitHub Actions job pulls RTHK stories, has an LLM rewrite each sentence into spoken Cantonese, runs a second review pass, adds jyutping over every character and records neural zh-HK audio. The app shows the written and spoken versions side by side and grades you character by character when you read aloud. It also has 96 scenario dialogues, from a cha chaan teng breakfast to an earnings call.

The problem

You can read the paper and still miss the evening news

In Hong Kong you read one register and hear another: newspapers are in written Chinese, while the evening news is spoken in Cantonese, with different words and different grammar. A learner can manage the first and still lose the thread of the second. The app puts both on screen and makes the mapping between them the thing you study.

Tap to compare

Tap a phrase and its counterpart lights up

The rewrite produces aligned phrase pairs. Tap an underlined phrase in either pane and its counterpart highlights in the other, so you can see that 認為 became 覺得. With the free tier, the pairs come from a character-level longest-common-subsequence aligner; the Claude tier returns them directly in its structured output.

On a phone the two panes become an interleaved view, with each written line directly above its spoken version.

Quality

A rewrite ladder with a review pass

Mechanical written-to-spoken conversion makes errors a learner can't spot (single-character rules turn 不足 into 唔足 and 其他 into 其佢), so the build uses the best tier available:

Claude
With an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY secret: a rewrite that returns aligned phrase pairs, then an independent verifier call. The day is labeled llm+verify
GitHub Models
The default, free: GPT-4.1 with a few-shot prompt that asks for restructured spoken Cantonese, falling back to GPT-4.1-mini when the daily quota runs out, then a GPT-4.1-mini review pass that may not make a line less colloquial. Calls are serialized with back-off and a per-model quota circuit breaker
Rules
The last resort, rebuilt after an audit of real output: phrase whitelists in place of single-character swaps (不 to 唔, 這 to 呢), quoted names in 「」 and 《》 left alone, sentence-final 了 to 喇

Numbers

Every numeral spelled out in context

In V1, Arabic numerals had no jyutping and no reliable reading. V2's numbers.js, shared by the build and the browser, reads each number the way an anchor would. The grader runs the recognizer's output through the same engine, so a correct reading scores green whether Chrome hears 二零二零年 or 2020年.

The curriculum

Eight categories of conversations you will have

Next to the news are 96 dialogues in 8 categories of 12: eating and drinking, getting around, shopping, friends and small talk, office life, finance and deals, home and services, and health and weather. Each line has written Chinese, spoken Cantonese with jyutping, an English gloss, a speaker label, and audio in a different voice per speaker. The source is hand-authored in content/conversations-src.json and was checked by separate AI review passes for natural Cantonese and for agreement between the three layers. The build script enforces 12 scenarios per category, no digits, and no Cantonese-only characters in the written layer.

Architecture

No server: a daily build and static files

GitHub Actions is the cron job and the build server. At 06:00 Hong Kong time it fetches RTHK RSS across five topics and the full article bodies, rewrites and reviews them, spells out numbers, adds jyutping with the to-jyutping library, synthesizes an MP3 per sentence with edge-tts zh-HK voices, and publishes frontend/ to GitHub Pages. Every step fails soft: no key means rules, a failed fetch means bundled sample lessons, a failed clip means the browser voice for that sentence.

Up to four lesson slots go to a second source: a WeChat RSS bridge if one is configured, otherwise a scrape of Google News for Chinese-language Bain Capital coverage. backend/junk.js filters reload banners, disclaimers and paywall prompts, and an article left with nothing but boilerplate is dropped. The repo has unit tests with node:test and a Playwright end-to-end test that renders the app headless.

Limitations

Chrome for grading, and a machine in the loop

Grading uses the browser's Web Speech API with the yue-Hant-HK locale, so it needs Chrome; other browsers can listen and read. In Chrome that API sends the recording to Google's speech service for recognition.

The spoken Cantonese in news lessons is machine-written and machine-reviewed. The review pass and the rebuilt rule converter cut errors without removing them all. Treat it as practice material, and trust your ear when a line sounds wrong.

News lessons are rebuilt every day and not archived. Space-separated digit groups such as a phone number written "2846 3222" are currently read as two cardinals; only unbroken runs are read digit by digit.
